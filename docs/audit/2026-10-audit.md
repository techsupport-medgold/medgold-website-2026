# Med Gold Health Care — standards audit

**Date:** 18 October 2026
**Live site:** https://medgoldhealthcare.com
**Code:** `techsupport-medgold/website` at the time of the audit, plus the fixes in this change
**Method:** read of the App Router codebase, HTTP inspection of the live site (HTML, headers, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`), a production build served locally, and a read-only look at the owner's other repos.

Scores are out of 10. "Before" is the live site and `main` on 18 October 2026. "After" is this branch. A 10 means the area matches the shared baseline and has nothing material left for a solo founder to schedule.

## What the product is

Med Gold (MedGold Healthcare) is a Chennai hospital-operations partner, not a clinic that treats patients. The public site sells five services to hospitals, clinics and pharmacies, and recruits clinical staff:

| Audience | What they need | Where the site serves them |
|---|---|---|
| Hospital directors and owners | NABH-aligned operations, staffing, pharmacy control, branding, patient feedback | Home, five service pages, contact |
| Pharmacy owners | Stock audit, expiry and leakage control | Pharmacy stock audit |
| Nurses and clinical staff | Jobs and training | Careers (resume upload), nursing training |
| The founder, Mr. Sundaramoorthy | Enquiries and applications in one inbox | Contact and careers forms |

The company is at Santosh Nagar, Kolathur, Chennai 600099, and says it covers Chennai and Kanchipuram. Phone, WhatsApp and `sundharmedgold@gmail.com` are the contact paths.

There is no separate backend and no database. Pages are static React Server Components. The contact and careers forms are Next.js server actions. They validate on the server, then send two emails through Gmail SMTP (`nodemailer`): one to `MAIL_TO` and one acknowledgement to the person who wrote in. A honeypot field drops obvious bots. Microsoft Clarity and Google Tag Manager (`GTM-P8G73P5Q`) are the only third-party scripts. Maps is an embed.

Stack: Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 3, Sass, Vitest. Deployed on Vercel from `main`. Canonical host is the apex; `www` 301s to it.

**Overall:** about 7.3 before, about 8.3 after. The foundation was already the shared playbook. The gaps were a few real SEO bugs, missing guards, and no CI.

### SEO — 7.0 → 8.5

Already in place on the live site: unique titles, canonicals on indexable pages, `robots.txt` allowing `/` and pointing at the sitemap, `www` 301 to the apex, Open Graph and Twitter cards, a generated 1200×630 image, web manifest, `MedicalOrganization` + `WebSite` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD, one `h1` per page checked, self-hosted fonts via `next/font`. Thank-you (`?status=success` and `?status=failed`), case studies and the 404 were already `noindex, follow` and absent from the sitemap. The style guide was `noindex, nofollow`.

Problems found on the live HTML:

- Most pages inherited one Open Graph title, `Hospital Healthcare Operations Management and Maintenance Company Chennai India`, instead of their own title. Home and About, the two pages that set their own `openGraph` block, had **no** `og:image`.
- Homepage description was 98 characters and read "…services in Chennai India". About was 76. Contact was 123 and ungrammatical ("Contact MedGold Healthcare send an enquiry").
- About, Careers, Contact, Privacy and Terms titles rendered under 35 characters with the `| Med Gold` suffix.
- Root keywords were clinic-template leftovers (`doctors`, `diagnostics`, `health checkup`) for a B2B operations company.
- `WebPage` JSON-LD existed only on Home and About. Service, contact, careers and legal pages did not have it.
- `MedicalOrganization` had no `areaServed`, even though the copy names Chennai and Kanchipuram.
- The code fallback when `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` is missing was `https://medgold.com`. Production is set correctly (the live sitemap uses `medgoldhealthcare.com`); a missed env var would have published the wrong host.
- A few service titles still render past 60 characters once `| Med Gold` is added (branding is about 68). They were left alone so keyword-rich titles that are already indexed do not get shortened in this pass.

### Performance — 8.0 → 8.2

`next/image` serves the hero as AVIF when the browser asks for it: about 10 KB at 640w, 20 KB at 1080w, 25 KB at 1920w, from a 162 KB JPEG source. Fonts are `display: swap` and preloaded as woff2. `/_next/static` is cached for a year as immutable outside development. Pages other than `/thank-you` are statically generated.

The HTML document is about 162 KB, which is the App Router flight payload as much as the visible markup. `/images/*` was `max-age=0` on the live host. There is no field Core Web Vitals number in the repo (no CrUX pull, no Lighthouse budget). This audit did not run Lighthouse.

### Security — 6.5 → 8.2

Already solid: `poweredByHeader: false`, HSTS with preload, `nosniff`, `SAMEORIGIN`, `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, a tight Permissions-Policy, honeypot, server-side validation, HTML escaping in emails, subject lines stripped of newlines, secrets only in env (`.env*` gitignored, `.env.example` has an empty app password), resume type and 4 MB cap, server actions body limit 5 MB.

Gaps: no Content-Security-Policy (GTM's inline bootstrap makes a strict policy easy to get wrong, so it was not added blindly). No rate limit, so a script could send mail until Gmail throttled it. `NEXT_PUBLIC_GTM_ID` was interpolated into an inline script with no shape check. Resume filenames were passed through to the attachment. Enquiry type, position and experience were not checked against the option lists. `npm audit` reported high issues in Next.js 16.0.0–16.3.7 (including an image-optimizer SSRF) and in `sharp`.

`npm audit fix` (no `--force`) moved Next.js to 16.4.2 and updated `sharp` and `source-map-js`. What remains is `braces` / `micromatch` / `postcss-selector-parser` under Tailwind 3 and ESLint. The only "fix" npm offers downgrades `eslint-config-next` to 14, which would be a break. Those stay on the roadmap.

### Accessibility — 8.0, unchanged

Reviewed, not retested with a screen reader. The site layout has one `main`. Forms have visible labels, `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`, 44px targets, and a honeypot that is hidden accessibly. Decorative icons use `aria-hidden`. Focus rings and `prefers-reduced-motion` are global. Contrast tokens are documented (gold is not used as text on white). `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y` is loaded but the raised rules are `warn`, so a new violation would not fail CI. There is no skip link, on purpose (project rule).

### Code quality — 7.5 → 8.5

The AVR layout is already in place: `src/app/(site)`, `src/data`, `src/config`, `src/lib`, small section components, strict TypeScript, 8 test files before this change. There was no CI and no `typecheck` script, so a red build could still merge. Sitemap membership and `noindex` were two hand-maintained lists.

### Developer experience — 7.0 → 8.5

README, `.env.example` and `.cursor/rules` (SEO, UI, no skip-link) are good. The documented production origin said `medgold.com`. There was no GitHub Actions workflow.

## Fixes applied

Safe changes only. Page layout, section copy and the form fields a person sees are the same. `npm run lint`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm test` (86 tests) and `npm run build` pass. A local production server confirmed the HTML.

1. **Open Graph and Twitter titles follow the page title**, including the `| Med Gold` template. Home and About no longer replace the metadata block in a way that dropped `og:image`. Every page checked now has `og:image` pointing at `/opengraph-image`.
2. **Snippet length.** Homepage, About and Contact descriptions are 140–160 characters. About, Contact, Careers, Privacy and Terms titles render at 50–60 characters with the brand suffix. A unit test locks those lengths. Contact's description is grammatical.
3. **Keywords** describe hospital operations, NABH audits, staffing, pharmacy audit, nursing training, patient feedback and branding.
4. **`WebPage` JSON-LD** on every indexable page (services, careers, contact, privacy, terms), via `PageSchema`.
5. **`MedicalOrganization`** now includes `areaServed` (Chennai, Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu) and the founder named on the About page.
6. **Canonical fallback** is `https://medgoldhealthcare.com` in `site.ts`, `next.config.ts`, `.env.example` and the README.
7. **`noindex, follow` is one constant** (`NOINDEX_FOLLOW`) used by thank-you (success and failure share the page), case studies, the style guide and the 404. The style guide moved from `nofollow` to `follow`, matching the other non-indexable pages. `/thank-you`, `/case-studies` and `/style-guide` also send `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, follow`. They are not in the sitemap; the sitemap module throws if one is added, and a test covers it. There is no payment page on this site.
8. **Form handling.** Enquiry type, job position and experience must be one of the options on the form. Resume attachment names lose path segments and header characters. Each form allows 8 submissions per 10 minutes per address per server instance, then asks the person to wait. A real honeypot hit still returns a quiet success and does not consume the limit. The field is `medgold_hp`, not a name browsers autofill.
9. **Analytics IDs.** A `NEXT_PUBLIC_GTM_ID` or Clarity id that is not the expected shape falls back to the known id, so a bad value cannot break out of the inline script.
10. **`/images/*` cache** for one day, with stale-while-revalidate for a week, in production only. Dev stays uncached so replaced files show up.
11. **Dependencies.** Non-breaking `npm audit fix`. Next.js 16.4.2, plus patched `sharp` and `source-map-js`.
12. **CI.** `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs lint, typecheck, test and build on Node 22. `npm run typecheck` exists.
13. **Enquiry email.** The contact and careers forms send through Gmail SMTP (`smtp.gmail.com:465`) from `SMTP_USER` to `MAIL_TO`, then an acknowledgement to the visitor. There is no API route. The honeypot used to be named `company_website`. Browsers fill that, the action returned success, and no email was sent. A failed send used to leave the page for a generic thank-you. Failures now stay on the form in an alert. The SMTP client is created per send, times out instead of hanging, and logs only the error code and response. Vercel still needs `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_APP_PASSWORD` and `MAIL_TO` on Production, plus a Gmail App Password (2-Step Verification). No domain DNS is required while From is the Gmail address.

## Roadmap

Ordered for one founder. Each item is something that can ship without a team.

### P0 — do these before spending on ads

1. **Search Console and Bing.** The env hooks already exist (`NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_BING_SITE_VERIFICATION`). Add the tokens in Vercel, submit `https://medgoldhealthcare.com/sitemap.xml`, and check that the apex is the preferred host. This is an account task, not a code task.
2. **Google Business Profile** for the Kolathur address, with the same name, phone and map pin as the site. Ask two hospital clients for a public review. This is the fastest local-discovery win and costs nothing.
3. **Three real case studies, then index `/case-studies`.** The page is a placeholder and correctly `noindex`. One pharmacy audit (dead stock recovered, expiry loss stopped), one staffing fill-rate, one feedback recovery story. Use numbers the client has approved. Then remove the path from `NOINDEX_PATHS` and add it to the sitemap. This is the highest-leverage page the site does not have.
4. **Confirm the schema type.** `MedicalOrganization` is what the playbook emits, and it is still what the site emits. Med Gold does not treat patients. If Search Console shows no rich-result benefit after a month, switch the primary type to `ProfessionalService` (keep the address, geo and `areaServed`). Do not invent opening hours or star ratings.

### P1 — trust and inbound, still a one-person build

5. **A short FAQ on each service page, with `FAQPage` JSON-LD only where the questions are visible.** Five questions a medical director actually asks (NABH edition, how fast a nurse can be on site, what a pharmacy audit includes, what data the feedback system stores). This is the healthcare SEO feature with the best ratio of effort to rich results.
6. **Content-Security-Policy.** Inventory the GTM container first (Analytics, Ads, Clarity), then ship an enforcing policy in a preview deploy and click WhatsApp, the map, both forms and a page view in Clarity. Report-only first if the container is noisy. Do not copy a strict policy onto production without that check.
7. **Durable rate limit and a transactional mailbox.** The in-memory limit dies with the serverless instance. When mail volume matters, put Upstash (or Vercel's firewall) in front of the two actions, and move off a Gmail app password to Resend or Postmark using `care@medgoldhealthcare.com`. Keep the two-email behaviour.
8. **NABH / pharmacy checklist as a lead.** A 2-page PDF ("12 checks before a pharmacy inspection") delivered by the existing enquiry form. No new backend. This matches how hospital owners actually buy.
9. **Cal.com or a WhatsApp prefill for "book an audit".** The contact form already collects the enquiry type. A calendar link for directors, and a `wa.me` link whose text names the service, is enough. Do not build a booking database.

### P2 — when the first five clients are steady

10. **Field Core Web Vitals.** Turn on the GA4 web-vitals tag inside the existing GTM container, or check CrUX in Search Console. Recompress only the images that show up as LCP problems. The hero is already ~20 KB AVIF at desktop width.
11. **Tamil block on the homepage and contact page.** One short section, not a second site. Many Kolathur and Kanchipuram buyers search in Tamil. Keep URLs in English so the current canonicals stay put.
12. **Staff verification page.** A hospital asks "did you train this nurse?". A lookup by certificate id, stored as a static JSON file the founder edits, is enough. No accounts.
13. **Raise jsx-a11y warnings to errors** and add `@axe-core/playwright` on home, contact and careers. Fix whatever it finds before turning the check on in CI.
14. **Page registry.** One typed list that drives `Routes`, the sitemap, nav and `PageSchema`, so a new service cannot ship without a canonical and a schema.
15. **Do not build an EHR, a patient login, or a feedback product inside this repo.** The feedback service page can keep selling the integration. Patient data belongs in a separate, access-controlled system. The privacy policy should be read by a lawyer before that system exists; the marketing site should keep collecting only enquiry and career data.

### Explicitly not in this change

- No visual redesign, no new sections, no copy changes inside the page body.
- No Content-Security-Policy (see P1).
- No `--force` audit fixes.
- Case studies stay `noindex` until real stories exist.
- Service titles that already rank were not shortened to chase a 60-character cutoff.
